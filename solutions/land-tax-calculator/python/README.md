# LBTT

## Bands and tax rates

- Up to £145,000 = 0%
- £145,001 to £250,000 = 2%
- £250,001 to £325,000 = 5%
- £325,001 to £750,000 = 10%
- Over £750,000 = 12%

## Example calculation

- House costs £1,000,000.
- First £145,000 gets taxed at 0% for £0 tax.
- The next £105,000 between £145,000 and £250,000 gets taxed at 2%, for £2,100 tax.
- The next £75,000 between £250,000 and £325,000 gets taxed at 5%, for £3,750 tax.
- The next £425,000 between £325,000 and £750,000 gets taxed at 10%, for £42,500 tax.
- The remaining £250,000 that is above £750,000 gets taxed at 12%, for £30,000 tax.
- Adding up all the tax brings us to £78,350 total.
